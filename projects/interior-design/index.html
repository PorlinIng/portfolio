Interior Design & Renovation

A portfolio of interior design 3D visualisations across living, dining, kitchen, bedroom, children's bedroom, and classroom spaces — each scheme balancing material warmth with functional clarity.

A collection of interior design 3D visualisations spanning residential and educational typologies, demonstrating Porlin’s range across space types, styles, and client briefs.

Living & Family Room Three distinct living room schemes: a luxury open-plan with marble flooring, integrated TV wall and backlit shelving; an elegant neutral composition with layered textiles, warm pendant lighting, and a rose-gold screen partition; and a contemporary Scandinavian-influenced family room with timber flooring, a green accent study wall, and relaxed furniture.

Living, Dining & Kitchen Open-plan kitchen and dining compositions including a high-contrast dark grey kitchen with marble island bench and wine storage, and a light airy dining room with steel-framed glass partition, marble dining table, and warm pendant cluster.

Bedroom Multiple adult bedroom schemes: a soft blush master suite with built-in vanity and full-height wardrobe; a warm timber-clad bedroom with upholstered panel headboard; and a contemporary dark-floor bedroom with wood-slat joinery and integrated study desk. Each scheme demonstrates spatial planning, material coordination, and lighting design.

Kid Bedroom Themed children’s bedrooms from playful to sophisticated: a teal moon-light bedroom with scalloped headboard on a raised platform; a pink cloud room with arched niche and walk-in wardrobe; and a bright gender-neutral room combining pastel blue, yellow, and pink with cloud-shaped mirror and study zone.

Class Room Two classroom typologies designed for learning environments: a modern studio classroom with round collaborative tables, green accent walls, and a recording setup; and an open-plan workshop classroom with maker benches, tool storage, and flexible seating for project-based learning.
