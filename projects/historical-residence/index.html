Historical Residence Renovation

Conserving 1960s Khmer modernism through a compact 'Extra Box' insertion that transforms between study and sleeping space using a dynamic sliding skin.

Architectural Competition Entry, 2019

A competition entry for the renovation of a 1960s residential block in Teok Thla commune, Sen Sok district, Phnom Penh — part of a housing estate originally designed by Cambodia’s celebrated architect Vann Molyvann for National Bank of Cambodia employees.

History In 1967, the National Bank of Cambodia commissioned affordable housing so employees could own their homes through monthly payments. The buildings — designed by Vann Molyvann — reflect the modernist Khmer architecture of the 1960s, combining natural ventilation strategies, chimney elements, French colonial influences, and traditional wooden Khmer house forms. By 1975–1979 all buildings were abandoned during the war. By 2016: 75% of units had been updated by new owners, 15% completely changed, 7% abandoned, and only 3% remained as free land. This rapid informal change is destroying the historical site.

Design Approach Rather than wholesale replacement, the proposal conserves the original Vann Molyvann structure and inserts a compact “Extra Box” addition — just 18m² — into the living area on the first floor. The Extra Box is a multifunctional space that transforms between uses:

  • Daytime: Open living and study space with dynamic skin open
  • Night: Three private sleeping zones using sliding doors — daughter’s bedroom, son’s bedroom, and guest room

The Dynamic Skin System The Extra Box has two skins working together:

  1. Glass skin — fixed outer layer for enclosure
  2. Dynamic skin — sliding screen that can open fully, close partially, or close fully, controlling privacy, shading, and spatial configuration

Site & Programme

  • Plot: 16m Wide × 27m Long
  • Existing house: 6.7m Wide × 10.9m Long (Ground Floor, First Floor, Roof)
  • Target family: 4 people — parents + 5-year-old daughter + 7-year-old son
  • Master plan additions: pool/pond, garden, basketball court, greenhouse, outdoor living, storage

Full construction drawings were produced including floor plans at 1:50, ceiling plan at 1:50, building sections at 1:100, and interior perspective renders of the Extra Box in both day and night configurations.
