Shophouse Proposal

Two shophouse exterior design proposals reimagining the urban terrace typology through timber screens, stacked volumes, and contemporary rhythm.

Two shophouse exterior design proposals exploring contemporary interpretations of the traditional Southeast Asian shophouse form.

City Frame is a premium terrace scheme with a strong brand identity — three units unified by a deep timber screen overhang and projecting white-rendered volumes at the upper floor. The ground level is open for retail activation, while decorative perforated panels at the first floor add texture and privacy.

The second scheme presents a four-storey shophouse row with a lighter tropical character: horizontal timber louvres shade each floor, hanging planter boxes introduce greenery at every level, and a top-floor transparent canopy brings natural light deep into the upper units. The composition balances rhythm and individuality across the terrace.

Both proposals demonstrate Porlin’s capability to design street-facing commercial architecture with a considered balance of commercial function, visual identity, and material warmth.

Interior Design The shophouse scheme extends into the interior, with 3D visualisations across the living and family room, dining and kitchen, and living spaces — demonstrating Porlin’s ability to take a project from street facade through to interior finish.
