Commercial Building

Two towers wrapped in a sinuous Kroma-patterned timber screen — a mixed-use high-rise that weaves Apsara dancer form and Khmer textile identity into central Phnom Penh's skyline.

Architectural Competition Entry

A mixed-use commercial and residential tower competition entry sited in central Phnom Penh, on a 65m × 70m plot (4,550m²) adjacent to Sangha Hotel, Amari Hotel, and ABC Apartment — bridging the city’s new urban development zone and its historic core.

Concept The scheme draws from two Khmer cultural references fused into a single architectural identity. The massing is shaped by the silhouette of the Apsara dancer — Cambodia’s most iconic figure — while the facade skin is inspired by the Kroma, the traditional Khmer textile worn by people across the country. The Kroma’s woven grid pattern is translated into a sinuous, undulating timber screen that wraps both towers, creating a facade that shifts in depth and rhythm as light moves across it throughout the day.

Programme Two towers — Building A (20 floors) and Building B (25 floors) — united by a shared podium:

  • Ground Floor: Small stores, coffee shops, local food restaurants, fast food, lobby for office & condo
  • 1st–3rd Floor: Shops, coffee shops, restaurants, cinema, art gallery
  • 4th–11th Floor: Head office, offices for rent, conference hall
  • 12th–13th Floor: Indoor garden, restaurant & bar
  • Building A, 14th–20th Floor: 18 × 3-bedroom units, 6 × 2-bedroom units
  • Building B, 15th–24th Floor: 22 studio units, 33 × 1-bedroom units, 11 × 2-bedroom units
  • Building A, 21st Floor: Gym and swimming pool
  • Building B, 26th Floor: Rooftop garden for residential use
  • Underground (2 levels): Parking for 68 + 72 cars, motorbike parking

A detailed physical scale model was produced showing the tower composition, the Kroma screen system, podium landscaping, and the site’s street-level activation.
