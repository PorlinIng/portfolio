An exterior facade study for a mid-rise office and residential tower, presenting elevation variants in white render. Each iteration tests a different rhythm of projecting balcony slabs, window proportions, and cascading vertical greenery — exploring how the facade reads from street level and at distance.

This proposal demonstrates Porlin's capacity for iterative facade design, delivering multiple developed options within a single design study.
