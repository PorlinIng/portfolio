A 3D exterior visualisation for a private luxury residence drawing from European classical architecture. The composition is anchored by a grand arched entrance framed by Corinthian-style pilasters, a second-floor balcony with ornamental balustrades, and symmetrical facade panels with circular motifs.

The scheme is rendered in pristine white, allowing the depth of the mouldings and the interplay of shadow at dusk and daylight to carry the facade's character. Mature tropical palms and accent planting soften the perimeter wall and entry forecourt, integrating the classical language into its Cambodian setting.

This project demonstrates Porlin's command of classical architectural grammar and high-quality photorealistic rendering for residential clients.
