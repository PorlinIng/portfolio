A 3D exterior visualisation series for a private vacation house designed for leisure and relaxation. The two-storey scheme is organised around a generous pool terrace, with the ground level opening fully to the water through large sliding glass panels and a covered timber-deck dining area.

The facade mixes white render with warm timber louvre screens and a distinctive red-lacquered front door — a playful accent within the otherwise cool palette. Stone-finish terrace paving, tropical planting in raised planters, and a rooftop garden create a layered outdoor sequence from arrival to pool to sky.

Four exterior views — pool elevation, entry facade, rear terrace, and garden corner — demonstrate Porlin's skill in capturing lighting conditions, material warmth, and the spatial relationship between architecture and landscape.
