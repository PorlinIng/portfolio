Baitong Hotel & Resort is a built hospitality project realized with ASMA Architects. The building is defined by a vertical concrete louvre facade densely threaded with tropical planting, creating a living screen that softens the street edge and filters light into the guest floors behind.

At the heart of the scheme is a dramatic courtyard pool — a long lap pool flanked by a cascading waterfall, palm trees, and layered greenery on every balcony face. The perforated masonry screen wrapping the courtyard creates a mosaic of light and shadow throughout the day, drawing guests into a sheltered garden world within the city.

The lobby continues this material warmth: a marble reception counter, geometric patterned tile climbing the open staircase wall, and a double-height void open to the garden beyond. The rooftop lounge captures Phnom Penh's skyline under a slatted timber canopy, with low lounge seating and a full bar — an evening destination as much as a hotel amenity.

Porlin contributed to the project through the architectural and construction phases, undertaking on-site coordination of facade elements and external works.
