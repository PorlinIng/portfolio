A facade renovation project for an existing link house, presented as a before-and-after study. The existing building photo shows the house in mid-construction — stripped facade, exposed structure, and an active renovation site — giving an honest view of the starting point.

The proposed design refreshes the street facade with a composed palette of white render and warm terracotta orange accent panels at the upper levels, decorative window awnings with terracotta tile inserts, aluminium-framed glazing, and a timber front door. The result transforms a generic urban house into a distinctive residence with warmth and character.

This project demonstrates Porlin's applied experience working with real existing buildings — understanding constraints, documenting existing conditions, and producing a resolved facade design that is buildable and contextually appropriate.
