A series of exterior facade design and renovation proposals illustrating Porlin's design concepts and 3D rendering skills across office and residential building typologies.

The first scheme redesigns a compact urban office building with a blue and white facade system — framed balconies with horizontal fins, integrated greenery on every floor, and a timber-clad ground entry. The design brings identity and visual interest to a narrow mid-rise footprint.

The second series explores facade options for a taller residential tower, presenting three elevation studies in white render. Each variant introduces a different rhythm of window openings, projecting balcony slabs, and cascading vertical greenery to test how the facade reads from street level and at distance.

Together these proposals demonstrate proficiency in 3D exterior visualisation, facade composition, material selection, and the integration of biophilic elements into contemporary urban buildings.
