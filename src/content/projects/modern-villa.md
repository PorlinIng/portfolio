A 3D exterior visualisation for a contemporary two-storey family villa. The facade combines grey textured stone cladding with large floor-to-ceiling glazed openings, slim dark-framed windows, and a flat roof profile — a restrained palette that lets the lush tropical planting and natural light do the expressive work.

The forecourt is finished in grey concrete paving with informal stone boulders and low tropical planting at the entry steps. The rear elevation opens to an outdoor living terrace, connecting the interior to the garden with the same material consistency.

This project illustrates Porlin's ability to visualise contemporary residential architecture at a high level of realism — capturing material texture, lighting, and landscape in a single cohesive exterior study.
