About

Porlin is an architect and designer with a practice spanning residential, hospitality, and civic scales. The work is rooted in a belief that architecture should speak the language of its place while aspiring toward timeless clarity.

Trained at the Royal University of Fine Arts in Phnom Penh, and with professional experience at ASMA Architects, Porlin brings a considered approach to form, material, and spatial storytelling — from large-scale hospitality architecture to facade composition and interior environments.
